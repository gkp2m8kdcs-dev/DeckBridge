Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play DeckBridge?

Swap cards to connect neighbouring cards of the same colour and form a smooth height gradient. When a complete path from START to FINISH is built, the toy car drives across to confirm your bridge works. The three lesson boards in the workshop walk you through the basics.

How do I use the Photo Studio?

Open Photo Studio, pick a photo from your library or take one with the camera, then choose a suit and a value. Your image becomes the card art for every playthrough. All photos are stored locally on your device — nothing is uploaded.

Does DeckBridge require an internet connection?

No. DeckBridge is fully offline: no accounts, no servers, no ads. Every feature works without a network connection.

How does two-player mode work?

Two-player pass-and-play lets two builders race the same bridge on one device. Each player gets separate move counters and timers, and a winner badge crowns the bridge master.

Where is my game progress stored?

All progress — stars, best moves, completion times, and saved bridge photos — is stored locally on your device in the Records tab. Nothing is synced or sent anywhere.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings and use the one-tap full reset. This erases all local progress, records, settings, and Photo Studio cards from the device. This action cannot be undone.

I found a bug. What should I include in my report?

Please email us with: your device model, iOS version, the level or feature where the problem occurred, steps to reproduce it, and a screenshot if possible.
