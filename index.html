Swap cards. Raise arches. Drive the bridge.

DeckBridge is a hand-crafted card puzzle where you swap card decks to raise and lower the arches of toy bridges. Each card carries a colour and a height. The colour binds neighbouring cards into one continuous bridge; the height controls how steep or gentle the slope is for your toy car. Build a complete, smooth path from START to FINISH and the car drives the entire route to confirm the bridge works.

Seven hand-tuned bridges

Advance across six environments — workshop floor, riverside, sky arch, skyline tower, deep canyon, cloud span, and a lantern-lit night market — each with its own palette and ambient detail.

Photo Studio

Turn your camera roll into card faces. Pick a photo or take one with the camera, choose a suit and value, and DeckBridge renders your image as the card art. All photos stay on your device.

Learn at your own pace

Three-star par scoring, instant undo, restart, and a hint overlay on every level — casual players are always welcome.

Two-player pass-and-play

Race the same bridge with separate move counters and timers. A winner badge crowns the bridge master.

Records tab

Track every completion: total stars, best moves per bridge, completion times, and saved bridge photos.

Truly private

No accounts, no analytics, no servers, no ads. Everything you make is stored locally on your device — and never sent anywhere.
