Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

In short: DeckBridge works entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers.

1. Overview

DeckBridge ("the App") is a self-contained card puzzle game for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, where it lives, and your choices regarding it. If you have any questions, contact us at Sitikhodijah00232@gmail.com.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

DeckBridge does not collect, transmit, or store on any server:

  • Names, email addresses, or any account credentials (the App has no accounts or login)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, calendars, or other personal content
  • Usage analytics, crash telemetry, or advertising identifiers
  • Device identifiers or fingerprinting data

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored only on your device and never leaves it:

  • Game progress and records — completed bridges, star ratings, best move counts, completion times, and saved bridge photos shown in the Records tab.
  • Settings — your appearance, sound, and haptic preferences.
  • Photo Studio cards — photos you select from your library or capture with the camera to use as card faces. These images are rendered and stored locally within the App's private storage.

You can erase all locally stored data at any time using the one-tap full reset in Settings, or by deleting the App from your device.

4. Photo Library and Camera Access

The Photo Studio feature requests access to your photo library (to pick an existing image) and/or your camera (to take a new one). Access is used solely to create card faces inside the App. Photos are never uploaded, shared, or transmitted. You may revoke these permissions at any time in iOS Settings, and the rest of the App will continue to work without them.

5. No Third-Party Services

DeckBridge contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, no social media integrations, and no external network calls. The App functions fully offline.

6. Children's Privacy

DeckBridge does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). Because the App collects no data at all, it is safe for users of all ages.

7. Data Security

Because all data remains in the App's sandboxed, on-device storage, it is protected by the security mechanisms built into iOS, including device encryption when you use a passcode.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
